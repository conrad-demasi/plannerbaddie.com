FAQ

Good questions.

How is this different from other meal planners?

Most planners pick recipes one at a time. plannerbaddie plans the whole week together so your dinners share ingredients, then builds the shopping list and the Sunday prep from that. Less to buy, less to waste, less time at the stove.

Do I have to meal prep?

Nope. Prep is there if you want it. Skip it and every dinner still has its full step-by-step recipe on the Cook tab.

Can I change my week after I plan it?

Yes. Swap any night for a better fit, add another dinner, drag one to a different day, or start a fresh week. Your shopping list updates with it, and you can save weeks you love to repeat later.

What if I already have some ingredients?

Tell us what's usually in your kitchen and it goes into a "probably have" section, so your list is only what you need. Mark anything else you've already got and it stays off next week's list too.
